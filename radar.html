Red flags

Things circulating in this ecosystem right now that will take your money and give you nothing. We would rather lose a sale than let you walk into one of these.

Anyone selling you ARC tokens

ARC is NOT launched. Circle minted 10B at genesis but explicitly said the mint 'does not represent a commitment to launch the token publicly.' Any pre-sale, presale allocation, or 'early ARC' offer right now is selling you something that does not exist. This is the single most likely scam in the Arc ecosystem today.

'Guaranteed yield on USDC'

Earn vaults are DeFi lending positions. They can lose money. Non-custodial means nobody refunds you.

Anyone promising to get you a grant for a fee

Grants are free to apply for. Circle does not use paid intermediaries.

Want us to just build it?

Reading the radar is free. Shipping is the hard part. If you would rather skip to the end, tell us what you need and we'll scope it in one call.
