What we will not do

This is the part most people in crypto skip, and it is why we are worth paying.

The ARC token is not launched.

Circle minted 10 billion ARC at genesis and then said publicly that the mint does not represent a commitment to launch the token publicly. The whitepaper itself says ARC is not launched.

So: anyone offering you ARC tokens, an ARC allocation, or "early access" right now is selling something that does not exist. We will never do that, and if a page you find through us ever does, tell us and we will pull it.

  • No token sales. We do not sell, allocate, or advise on any token.
  • No guaranteed returns. The Earn vaults in Arc's SDK are DeFi lending positions. They can lose money.
  • No "pay us to get you a grant". Grants are free to apply for. Circle uses no paid intermediaries.
  • Disclosed affiliate links. Where we earn a referral fee, we say so on the link. Always.
